FAQ

How is this different from hiring an SDR?+
An SDR works 8 hours a day and can contact maybe 30–50 people. Our AI runs 24/7, contacts thousands with personalized messaging, never gets sick, and costs a fraction of a full-time hire. You get the output of a team of SDRs without the management overhead.
What industries do you work with?+
We specialize in B2B — SaaS, consulting, professional services, fintech, and agencies. If you sell to businesses and have a clear ICP, we can build a system that works for you.
How quickly will I see results?+
Most clients receive their first qualified meeting within 2–3 weeks of going live. Measurable pipeline impact typically shows within 30–45 days. We publish transparent reporting so you see progress in real time.
Is the outreach compliant with GDPR?+
Yes. We operate fully within GDPR and CAN-SPAM guidelines. All outreach targets business contacts with legitimate interest, includes proper opt-out mechanisms, and uses compliant data sources.
What do I need to get started?+
Just a clear picture of who your ideal client is and what you sell. We handle everything else — infrastructure, copywriting, targeting, setup, and ongoing optimization. No technical knowledge required.
